Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Meylo ("we", "us") collects when you use our app, how we use it, and the choices you have. Meylo is a dating app intended only for people 18 years of age or older.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Meylo, we collect:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password (stored securely by our authentication provider, Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your raw password).
  • Profile information: your date of birth (used to calculate and display your age), gender, city, bio, photos, and any details you choose to add (height, occupation, interests, etc.).
  • Voice intro: if you choose to record one, a short audio clip you provide.
  • Messages: the content of messages you send to other members through the app.
  • Usage data: actions like likes, profile visits, gifts sent, ads watched, and daily task activity, which we use to operate features like Daily Tasks and prevent abuse.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report or block another user, we record that action and the reason given.

We do not currently collect payment card details — paid features are marked "Coming Soon" until a licensed payment processor is integrated.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and operate your account and profile
  • To show your profile to other members and let you discover, match, and chat with them
  • To operate features like Daily Tasks, Lucky Spin, and Virtual Gifting
  • To investigate reports of abusive behavior and enforce blocks
  • To keep the app secure and prevent fraud or abuse of features (e.g. verifying ad-watch and task completion server-side)

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) and processed by serverless functions hosted on Vercel. These providers may store data on servers outside your country.

4. Other Members Can See

Your name, age, city, photos, bio, voice intro, and other profile details you choose to add are visible to other Meylo members. Your email address and password are never shown to other members.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can edit or remove most profile details (bio, photos, voice intro) at any time from My Profile.
  • You can block other members, which prevents mutual visibility and contact.
  • You can request that we delete your account and associated data by contacting us (see below).

6. Age Requirement

Meylo is for users 18 and older. We ask for your date of birth at signup to check this. We do not currently perform government-ID verification of age — please report any profile you believe belongs to someone under 18.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard practices including authenticated access control, server-side validation of sensitive actions (like coin balances and gifts), and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to report a concern, or want to request deletion of your data, contact us at meylosupport@gmail.com.
